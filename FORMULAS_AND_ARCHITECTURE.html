1. Philosophy & Glass-Box Explainability

Standard Battery Management Systems (BMS) in commercial electric micro-mobility rely on naive linear distance approximations based strictly on instantaneous battery State of Charge (SoC). In dense developing urban centers, these estimates fail severely due to unmodeled stop-and-go thermal stress, passenger overload (>450 kg), and road gradients.

E-Rakshak replaces opaque approximations with a Glass-Box Deterministic Energy Model. Every prediction shown on the Driver Dashboard and Trip Planner is backed by verifiable physical formulas, live parameter substitution, and dynamic multi-factor stress scaling.

3. Mathematical Foundations & Physics Formulations

Formula 1: Usable Battery Pack Energy (E_usable) Battery Management

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) and Lithium-ion cells cannot be depleted to 0.0V without irreversible copper shunting and degradation. The system allocates a 10% BMS safety buffer:

E_usable = C_pack × (SoC / 100) × η_usable
E_usable [Wh] = Pack Capacity [Wh] × (SoC % ÷ 100) × Usable Fraction (0.90)

Variables: C_pack = Total nameplate battery capacity (e.g. 7,370 Wh for Mahindra Treo); SoC = Current State of Charge (%); η_usable = 0.90 (10% lower cutoff buffer retained by BMS).

Formula 2: Dynamic Operating Energy Consumption (C_adj) Environmental Multipliers

Vehicle lab-certified consumption is scaled across four independent environmental and payload stress dimensions:

C_adj = C_nominal × M_load × M_temp × M_terrain × M_traffic
Adjusted Consumption [Wh/km] = Nominal Baseline × Multipliers
Multiplier Condition / Category Factor Value Physical / Electrochemical Rationale
Payload (M_load) Empty / Driver only 0.88× Reduced rolling resistance and inertial mass during acceleration.
Normal (1-2 Passengers) 1.00× Standard certified operating test load.
Heavy (3-4 Passengers) 1.25× Higher rolling drag and elevated mechanical powertrain strain.
Overloaded (>450 kg Cargo) 1.55× Severe inertial drag, tires operating under maximum rated deflection.
Thermal (M_temp) Cold (< 15°C) 1.15× Sluggish electrolyte ion motility, elevated cell internal resistance (R_int).
Optimal (15°C - 42°C) 1.00× Ideal electrochemical kinetic operating envelope.
Overheated (> 42°C) 1.18× Electrolyte breakdown, parasitic fan drain, elevated ohmic heat generation.
Terrain (M_terrain) Urban Flat 1.00× Negligible gravitational potential energy draw.
Rough / Broken Roads 1.15× Continuous micro-accelerations and suspension vibration dissipation.
Hilly (>80m Elevation Gain) 1.30× Direct gravitational work (m·g·Δh) converted against electric motor torque.
Traffic (M_traffic) Low / Free-Flow 1.00× Steady-state cruising speed with minimal braking dissipation.
Moderate Urban 1.08× Typical city traffic signal stops and commuter pedestrian pauses.
Heavy Congestion 1.22× Frequent stop-and-go cycles maximizing I²R resistive heating in motor coils.
Formula 3 & 4: Usable Range & Uncertainty Intervals Predictive Reach
R_usable = E_usable ÷ C_adj
Estimated Range [km] = Usable Energy [Wh] ÷ Adjusted Rate [Wh/km]
R_lower = R_usable × 0.85  |  R_upper = R_usable × 1.15
Statistical Confidence Band: ±15% variance reflecting aggressive throttle, headwind, and regenerative braking recovery.

4. Trip Reachability & Feasibility Evaluation Matrix

Formula 5, 6 & 7: Route Energy Demand, Safety Reserve & Total Budget Route Planning

For any planned route of distance D (calculated via TomTom Routing Engine):

E_demand = D [km] × C_adj [Wh/km]
Trip Energy Demand: Pure electrical draw required to reach destination
E_reserve = E_usable × 0.15
Safety Reserve: 15% of available usable battery held against unmapped detours, traffic jams, and signal idling
E_total = E_demand + E_reserve
Total Journey Energy Footprint
Formula 8: Reachability Verdict Classification Logic Decision Support
Classification Verdict Mathematical Evaluation Condition System Recommendation & Driver Protocol
LIKELY FEASIBLE E_demand + E_reserve ≤ E_usable Journey is well within battery capacity with a safe buffer. Maintain standard driving profile.
UNCERTAIN / TIGHT MARGIN E_demand ≤ E_usable < E_demand + E_reserve Fits in battery but eats into the 15% safety reserve. Unexpected detours or traffic could strand the vehicle.
NOT RECOMMENDED E_demand > E_usable Deficit detected. Battery will be exhausted before reaching destination. Mandatory stop at recommended charger.
Formula 9 & 10: Destination Arrival SoC & Congestion Index Telematics
Arrival_SoC (%) = max( 0, [ (E_usable - E_demand) / C_pack ] × 100 )
Predicted Destination Battery Percentage upon trip completion
Congestion_Ratio (CR) = ( T_live - T_free_flow ) / T_free_flow
Where: CR ≤ 0.10: Low Traffic | 0.10 < CR ≤ 0.35: Moderate Urban | CR > 0.35: Heavy Congestion
Formula 11: Emergency Zero-SoC Autonomous Watchdog Safety Critical

The background telemetry daemon monitors real-time battery voltage and State of Charge every 3 seconds:

TRIGGER:   ( SoC ≤ 0% )   OR   ( Battery_Temperature ≥ 55°C )
Autonomous dispatch of GPS Distress Payload via WhatsApp Direct API & SMS Gateway

5. Step-by-Step Worked Engineering Example

Operational Profile: Mahindra Treo E-Rickshaw in Varanasi Corridor Case Study
1
Given Vehicle Baseline Specifications:
Pack Capacity: 7,370 Wh (51.2V, 144Ah LFP) | Lab Efficiency: 78.0 Wh/km | Live SoC: 64% | Temp: 34.5°C
2
Calculate Usable Energy (E_usable):
7,370 Wh × (64 ÷ 100) × 0.90 = 4,245.12 Wh ≈ 4,245 Wh available.
3
Evaluate Dynamic Consumption Multipliers:
Normal Load (1.00×) • Temp 34.5°C (1.00×) • Flat Terrain (1.00×) • Moderate Traffic (1.08×)
C_adj = 78.0 × 1.00 × 1.00 × 1.00 × 1.08 = 84.2 Wh/km
4
Compute Usable Range & Uncertainty Band:
Nominal Range: 4,245 Wh ÷ 84.2 Wh/km = 50.4 km
Conservative Lower Bound: 50.4 × 0.85 = 42.8 km | Favorable Upper Bound: 50.4 × 1.15 = 58.0 km
5
Planned Corridor: IIT BHU Aryabhatta Hostel → Bhullanpur Varanasi (4.1 km):
Route Energy Demand: 4.1 km × 84.2 Wh/km (with safety headroom) = 598 Wh
Safety Reserve Buffer (15%): 4,245 Wh × 0.15 = 637 Wh
Total Energy Requirement: 598 Wh + 637 Wh = 1,235 Wh
6
Reachability Verdict & Destination Battery Percentage:
Feasibility Comparison: 1,235 Wh required < 4,245 Wh available → LIKELY FEASIBLE
Remaining Battery at Destination: 4,245 Wh - 598 Wh = 3,647 Wh
Arrival SoC: (3,647 Wh ÷ 7,370 Wh) × 100 = 49.48% ≈ 49% SoC
